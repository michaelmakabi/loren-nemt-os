# Client Demo Script — 30 minute structure

## Pre-call (T-15 min)
- Review their kickoff form answers
- Open ROI calculator with their numbers pre-loaded
- Open Godspeed live deployment in second tab
- Open their LinkedIn / company website for personalization

## 0:00 – 5:00 — Discovery (operator does 80% of the talking)
Open with: **"Before I show you anything — walk me through a typical Tuesday at {company name}. What do you see, what do you hear, what frustrates you?"**

Listen for:
- # of calls / day
- # missed
- # dispatchers
- Current tools (CRM, scheduler, phone)
- Recent contract wins / losses
- Owner's actual day (running dispatch personally? trying to step away?)

Quick clarifying questions:
- "What's an average ride value for you?"
- "How many vehicles?"
- "Any facility contracts? Dialysis? SNF?"
- "Who answers the phone when {dispatcher_name} is at lunch?"

## 5:00 – 15:00 — Show, don't tell
1. "Let me show you what one of our operators built — Godspeed Transportation in Detroit, 25 drivers."
2. Walk Godspeed homepage → call demo → dashboard preview → driver app.
3. **Translate to their world:** "Yours would look like this — your logo, your colors, your phone number, your patients. Same engine, fully branded."

## 15:00 – 22:00 — Their math, on screen
Open ROI calculator with their numbers pre-loaded. Walk slowly.

> "You said you get 80 calls a day. At a 15% miss rate, that's 12 missed. At an $80 ride, that's $960 a day. Five days a week. That's almost $250,000 a year leaking out the door."

Pause. Let it land.

> "Loren AI catches those. Even if we only catch half — that's $125,000/yr. Our Growth tier is $42,000/yr fully loaded. You'd recover 3x the cost in year one."

## 22:00 – 27:00 — Offer + guarantee
> "Here's how it works. $4,500 to deploy. We white-label everything in under 72 hours. $3,497 a month after that. If we don't capture at least 3 extra rides a day in your first 60 days, we refund the setup fee. No fine print."

Handle objections:
- "Need to talk to my partner" → "Totally fair. Want me to send a 90-second Loom you can share?"
- "Sounds too good" → "Look at Godspeed. Call Shihada. I'll connect you."
- "Too expensive" → "Compare to one dispatcher: $4,000/mo, sleeps, takes weekends. We're cheaper and don't."
- "Need to think" → "Of course. Can I send you the custom ROI report by tonight?"

## 27:00 – 30:00 — Close or schedule next step
**The close:**
> "Three ways to leave this call: (1) you want in, I send the Stripe link now; (2) you want me to send the ROI report and circle back Friday; (3) it's not the right time, no hard feelings. Which feels right?"

If they say "now" → send Stripe link in real time → mark Closed-Won in GHL.
If they say "Friday" → calendar follow-up + ROI report.
If they say "no" → ask permission to revisit in 90 days.

## Post-call (T+5 min)
- Update GHL: stage move, notes, tier quoted, next step
- If closed: trigger Onboarding workflow
- If not closed: schedule next-touch automation
