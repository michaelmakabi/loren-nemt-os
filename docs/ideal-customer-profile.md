# Ideal Customer Profile

## Tier 1 — Bullseye

**Operator profile**
- Owns / runs an NEMT or medical transportation company
- 10–75 vehicles
- US-based, prioritizing: FL, TX, MI, OH, PA, GA, NC, AZ, CA
- 5–15 years in business
- Mix of Medicaid broker rides + private-pay + facility contracts
- Currently has 2–5 dispatchers / office staff
- Owner is operator (still touches the business daily)

**Pain signals (any 2+ qualify)**
- "We miss 10+ calls a day."
- "I had to hire another dispatcher and nothing changed."
- "I lost a facility contract because of complaints."
- "I want to take a vacation and I can't."
- "I'm running 4 different software tools and they don't talk."
- "My no-show rate is climbing."

**Buying triggers**
- Just lost a contract.
- Just hired a dispatcher.
- Got a Medicaid audit notice.
- Considering selling the business (wants to make it sellable).
- Wants to add a 2nd location.

**Budget posture**
- $2k–$10k/mo in operating software is achievable.
- Has been burned by dispatch SaaS that didn't deliver — needs proof.

## Tier 2 — Stretch

**Operator profile**
- Multi-location regional fleet (75–300 vehicles)
- Backed by PE or family-office capital
- Has a COO and a dedicated ops team

Slower close (3–6 months), bigger ACV ($7k–$15k/mo + custom).

## Tier 3 — Adjacent (post-PMF)

- Senior-living transportation
- Hospital discharge fleets
- VA medical transport
- Specialty paratransit

## Anti-ICP (do NOT pursue)

- Single-vehicle owner-operators (no budget, no ROI).
- The big 5 brokers themselves (build in-house).
- Pure rideshare / Uber Health subcontractors (too thin-margin).
- Anyone who says "we need to talk to IT first" (multi-month procurement, not worth it at our stage).

## Buyer personas

### Persona 1 — "Owner-Operator Otis"
- 45–62, second-generation owner or built it himself
- Drove a van for 5+ years before becoming owner
- Bought TripMaster in 2019, hates it but trapped
- Reads email at 6 AM and 9 PM
- Cares about: not losing his weekends, making the business sellable

**Pitch angle:** "Run your business from your phone. Sleep through the night."

### Persona 2 — "Operations Olivia"
- 38–50, hired as Director of Dispatch / Ops Manager
- Reports to an absentee owner or a PE-backed parent
- KPI-driven, wants to look good in board reviews
- Tech-comfortable, frustrated by legacy tools

**Pitch angle:** "Walk into your next board meeting with a real dashboard."

### Persona 3 — "Expansion Eric"
- 40–55, owns 2 locations, opening a 3rd
- Knows the current system won't scale
- Has a budget, wants speed
- Has been pitched 4 dispatch tools, none gave him a branded customer surface

**Pitch angle:** "Open location 3 with the platform your competitors don't have."

## Where to find them

- **Outbound channels:**
  - NEMTAC member directory (national association)
  - State Medicaid provider lists (public)
  - Modivcare / MTM contracted-provider directories
  - LinkedIn (job title: Owner / Operations Manager + industry: Ground Passenger Transportation)
  - Facebook NEMT operator groups (5+ active, 10k+ members combined)

- **Inbound:**
  - SEO target keywords: "NEMT dispatch software", "Medicaid transportation software", "NEMT AI receptionist"
  - YouTube: "How we recovered $X in missed rides" case-study content
  - Cold email via Instantly.ai, ~250/wk

## Disqualifiers (kill the deal early)

- Less than 5 vehicles (skip to "come back when you grow")
- "We just signed a 3-year contract with X" (revisit in 30 months)
- Owner doesn't take the demo personally (needs decision-maker in the room)
- No Stripe / payment-ready (post-2026 we don't accept checks)
