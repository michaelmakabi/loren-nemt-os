# Scaling Roadmap

## Target: 25 paying operators in 180 days, $87.5k MRR

## 30-day plan
- 3 operators live ($5,991 MRR + $7,500 setup)
- Cold email machine running: 250/wk, 4-step
- 1 case study v2 published (one of the new clients)
- Onboarding SOP refined to genuine <72hr SLA

## 60-day plan
- 8 operators live
- Add a Spanish-language Grace voice (huge for FL / TX / CA markets)
- Launch the driver-app PWA
- 5-touch nurture sequence for demo no-shows

## 90-day plan
- 15 operators live
- Open second outbound channel: LinkedIn (50/wk manual on Persona 1 & 2)
- Hire a part-time SDR
- Conference: book NEMTAC fall summit booth

## 180-day plan
- 25 operators live, $87.5k MRR blended
- Channel partnership signed with 1 of the 5 big brokers
- HIPAA BAA standard issue with every new contract
- First Fleet-tier ($6,997/mo) operator live
- Multi-location operator live (validates Phase 3 architecture)

| When | Move |

## Team unlocks

| Stage | Hire | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 5 ops | Part-time SDR | Outbound capacity |

## Defensibility moves to ship by day 180

1. NEMT-specific call dataset (10,000+ logged calls) → fine-tuned Retell agent.
2. HIPAA + BAA posture (legal review done, BAA template ready).
3. Direct broker EDI for at least 1 of the big 5.
4. White-label deployment SLA tightened from <72hr to <24hr (productized installer).
5. 3 case-study videos (Godspeed + 2 new operators) for outbound.

## North-star metrics

- **Operator NPS:** target 60+.
- **Captured-call-per-day average across the fleet of clients:** target 8+ (well above guarantee floor of 3).
- **Onboarding SLA hit rate:** 100%.
- **Annual gross revenue retention:** 95%+.
- **Annual net revenue retention:** 120%+ (driven by add-on numbers, voices, EDI).

## What kills this roadmap (and how we avoid it)

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Voice AI quality degrades on edge cases | NEMT-specific evals, weekly call sampling, fallback to dispatcher |
| Operator churns after 90 days | QBR cadence, captured-call KPI shown weekly, upsell pathway |
| Big broker bans white-label voice in their network | Pre-built broker compliance posture, BAA, signed broker MOUs |
| Lovable platform risk | Self-host fallback documented; codebase is portable React |
| Single-channel dependency (cold email) | LinkedIn + conference + referral by day 90 |
