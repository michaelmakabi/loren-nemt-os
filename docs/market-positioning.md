# Market Positioning — Loren AI for NEMT

## Market size & shape

- **US NEMT market:** ~$15B annually, growing ~9% CAGR through 2030 (aging population + Medicaid expansion).
- **Operators:** estimated 12,000–15,000 NEMT companies in the US, mostly small (5–50 vehicles).
- **Brokers:** dominated by ~5 firms (Modivcare/LogistiCare, MTM, Access2Care, Verida, SafeRide). Operators contract through them.
- **Reimbursement:** mostly Medicaid (state-by-state rates), some Medicare Advantage, some private-pay.

## Why this vertical, why now

1. **Operators are 20+ years behind on tech.** Most still run on phone trees, spreadsheets, and an Excel scheduler.
2. **Dispatcher labor cost is rising.** Post-2022 wage compression is permanent.
3. **AI voice is finally good enough.** Retell + ElevenLabs in 2026 sounds indistinguishable from a human for the call patterns NEMT actually receives.
4. **Medicaid scrutiny is increasing.** Audit-ready logging is now a requirement, not a nicety — we deliver it by default.
5. **Broker consolidation pressures margins.** Operators NEED to capture every non-broker ride to survive.

## Where we play

- **Segment 1 (primary): independent operators, 10–75 vehicles.** Owner is operator. Fastest to close, highest pain.
- **Segment 2: multi-location regional operators, 75–300 vehicles.** Slower sales cycle, higher ACV.
- **Segment 3 (later): broker contractors who need a "modern look" to win MCO bids.**

## Where we don't play (yet)

- Single-vehicle owner-operators (no budget).
- The big 5 brokers themselves (they build in-house).
- Pure paratransit / municipal contracts (different procurement cycle).

## Buyer profile

- **Title:** Owner / Operations Manager / Director of Dispatch.
- **Background:** typically came up driving or dispatching; bought / inherited the company.
- **Tech literacy:** medium-low; appreciates "show me, don't make me figure it out".
- **Trigger events:**
  - Lost a contract because of missed-call complaints.
  - Hired a 5th dispatcher and margins didn't move.
  - Broker added penalties for late arrivals / no-shows.
  - Owner is trying to step away from day-to-day for the first time.

## Competitive landscape

| Category | Players | What they get right | What they miss |
|---|---|---|---|
| NEMT dispatch SaaS | TripMaster, Routing Box, Tobi Cloud | Routing, EDI, broker integration | No AI voice. No customer-facing portal. No marketing site. |
| Horizontal AI receptionists | Smith.ai, Goodcall, Air.ai | Decent voice quality | No NEMT vocabulary. No dispatch handoff. No branded site. |
